================================================================================
LESSON GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS (LanguageStudio Platform)
================================================================================

When instructed to "create Lesson [N]" or "add Lesson [N] following these guidelines", 
generate a complete, production-ready lesson module adhering strictly to the 4-part 
structure detailed below.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CORE DIRECTIVES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. COMPREHENSIVE THEORY FIRST: Do not summarize or abbreviate grammar/phonetic rules. 
   Explain concepts deeply and thoroughly as a dedicated teacher would during a live class.
2. UKRAINIAN LANGUAGE FIRST: All theoretical explanations, translations, notes, 
   and pronunciation guides MUST be in Ukrainian (not in English). 
   Pronunciation must be provided using clear Ukrainian phonetic transcriptions 
   (e.g., [буон-джор-но], [ча-о]).
3. NO AUDIO & NO "РЕГІСТР": Do not include audio cues/triggers, and do not use 
   the "Регістр" classification. Focus on clear context, time of day, and situation.
4. ADAPTIVE CONTENT: Adapt the tone, vocabulary, dialogues, and exercise types 
   specifically to the topic of the lesson.
5. CLEAR PEDAGOGICAL FLOW: Move seamlessly from learning (Theory & Vocabulary) 
   to active practice (Drills & Interactive Games).

================================================================================
REQUIRED LESSON STRUCTURE
================================================================================

1. LESSON METADATA
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Lesson Number & Title (Italian & Ukrainian)
- CEFR Level (e.g., A0, A1, A2)
- Target Learning Objectives (3-4 concise bullet points in Ukrainian)
- Estimated Completion Time (e.g., 15–20 хв)

2. SECTION 1: IN-DEPTH THEORY & CONCEPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Detailed explanation of rules, patterns, and cultural nuance in Ukrainian.
- Comprehensive Reference Tables: Include gender, singular/plural, conjugations, 
  or Ukrainian phonetic breakdowns [вимова українською] where applicable.
- Pronunciation Guides: Highlight key sounds, accent marks, stress rules using 
  Ukrainian phonetic transcription (e.g., [буон-джор-но]).
- Common Pitfalls & Exceptions: Explicitly call out frequent student mistakes.

3. SECTION 2: REAL-WORLD EXAMPLES & DIALOGUES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Contextual Sentences: Provide isolated examples with verbatim Ukrainian translations.
- Authentic Conversations:
  * Provide at least two realistic dialogues (e.g., University Campus vs. Office/Legal scenario).
  * Format dialogues clearly with speaker names, Italian text, and line-by-line Ukrainian translations.
- Key Vocabulary Breakdown: Highlight essential keywords and expressions used 
  in the dialogues with Ukrainian translations.

4. SECTION 3: GUIDED PRACTICE DRILLS (3-4 EXERCISES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Provide a varied set of interactive exercises using the following formats (No audio):
- Exercise 1: Multiple Choice / Contextual Selection (Targeting grammar & situational rules).
- Exercise 2: Sentence Builder / Fill-in-the-Blank (Targeting spelling, word forms & context).
- Exercise 3: Translation / Reading Comprehension (Targeting contextual meaning & interpretation).
- Exercise 4: Sorting / Matching Exercise (Targeting courteous responses, vocabulary pairs).

*For every exercise, provide:*
- Clear instructions for the student in Ukrainian.
- The correct answer key.
- Pedagogical Explanation/Feedback for both correct and incorrect responses in Ukrainian.

5. SECTION 4: GAMIFIED REVIEW / CHECKPOINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Design a themed mini-game or interactive quiz mechanism tailored to the topic:
- Game Type (e.g., Time-Attack Categorization, Matching Pairs, Speed Flashcards).
- Game Rules & Win Condition in Ukrainian (e.g., "Сортуйте 10 фраз правильно за 45 секунд для отримання +50 XP").
- Item Pool: List of 8–12 vocabulary items or prompts used inside the game.
