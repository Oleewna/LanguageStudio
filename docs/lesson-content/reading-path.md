# Italian Course Reading Path

This file describes the order of the lessons in the Italian course and how the practice lesson fits into the sequence. Lesson content is in [communication-basics.md](communication-basics.md).

## Path: Communication basics

- **Path ID:** `foundations`
- **Course:** Italian (`it`)
- **Source:** `courses/it/course.json`
- **Description:** Introductions and everyday conversations

### Unit: First steps

1. **Lesson 1 — Introductions**
   - **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-1`
   - **Duration:** 20 min
   - **Content:** Greetings, names, countries, and starting a short conversation.
   - **Lesson text:** [communication-basics.md](communication-basics.md#lesson-1-introductions)

   **Extra practice — Introductions: more phrases**
   - **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-1-practice`
   - **Duration:** 8 min
   - **Content:** Introducing yourself, sharing personal details, and asking follow-up questions.
   - **Lesson text:** [communication-basics.md](communication-basics.md#lesson-2-introductions-more-phrases)

2. **Lesson 2 — Study, work, and languages**
   - **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-2`
   - **Duration:** 20 min
   - **Content:** Talking about study, work, languages, and family; asking simple questions.
   - **Lesson text:** [communication-basics.md](communication-basics.md#lesson-3-study-work-and-languages)

3. **Lesson 3 — Interests and everyday life**
   - **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-3`
   - **Duration:** 20 min
   - **Content:** Talking about interests and free time, and making plans to meet.
   - **Lesson text:** [communication-basics.md](communication-basics.md#lesson-4-interests-and-everyday-life)
