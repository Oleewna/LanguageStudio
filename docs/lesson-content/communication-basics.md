## Lesson 1: Introductions

- **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-1`
- **Source file:** `courses/it/lessons/communication-basics-1.json`
- **Next lesson:** `communication-basics-1-practice`

### Overview

- **Subtitle:** Come ti chiami?
- **Section label:** Communication basics · Lesson 1
- **Introduction:** Learn to greet someone, say your name and country, and start a short conversation.

### Dialogue: Classroom dialogue

- **Context:** Sofia arrives for her first Italian lesson in Milan and meets Greta, another student in the course.

- **Sofia:** Buongiorno!
  - **English:** Good morning!
  - **Pronunciation:** [bwon-JOR-no]

- **Greta:** Buongiorno! Come ti chiami?
  - **English:** Good morning! What is your name?
  - **Pronunciation:** [KOH-meh tee KYAH-mee]

- **Sofia:** Mi chiamo Sofia. E tu?
  - **English:** My name is Sofia. And you?
  - **Pronunciation:** [mee KYAH-moh SOH-fee-ah. eh too]

- **Greta:** Mi chiamo Greta. Piacere!
  - **English:** My name is Greta. Nice to meet you!
  - **Pronunciation:** [mee KYAH-moh GREH-tah. pyah-CHEH-reh]

### Vocabulary

| Italian | English |
| --- | --- |
| buongiorno | good morning / hello |
| come ti chiami? | what is your name? |
| mi chiamo | my name is |
| piacere | nice to meet you |

### Culture note

In Italian classrooms, lessons often begin with a greeting and a brief chat. It is more than a formality: this small exchange helps create a friendly atmosphere before the lesson begins.

### Key takeaways

- Buongiorno is a greeting used in the morning or during the day.
- Mi chiamo ... means “My name is ...”.
- Piacere is the standard way to say “Nice to meet you.”

- **Duration:** 8 min

### Vocabulary: Introduction examples

| Italian | English |
| --- | --- |

### Dialogue: Short dialogue

- **A:** Ciao! Come ti chiami?
  - **English:** Hi! What's your name?

- **B:** Mi chiamo Anna. E tu?
  - **English:** My name is Anna. And you?

- **A:** Sono Marco. Di dove sei?
  - **English:** I'm Marco. Where are you from?

- **B:** Vengo dall'Ucraina. Piacere!
  - **English:** I'm from Ukraine. Nice to meet you!

### Reading: Short profile

> Ciao! Mi chiamo Elena. Sono di Kyiv, ma vivo a Milano. Parlo ucraino e inglese. Studio italiano.
> English: Hi! My name is Elena. I'm from Kyiv, but I live in Milan. I speak Ukrainian and English. I'm studying Italian.

**Vocabulary hints**

- **Sono di** - I'm from
- **vivo** - I live
- **Parlo** - I speak

## Lesson 3: Study, work, and languages

- **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-2`
- **Source file:** `courses/it/lessons/communication-basics-2.json`
- **Previous lesson:** `communication-basics-1-practice`
- **Next lesson:** `communication-basics-3`

### Overview

- **Subtitle:** A pair activity
- **Description:** Learn to talk about study, work, languages, and family, and ask simple questions.
- **Duration:** 20 min
- **Experience reward:** 50 XP

### Dialogue: Pair-work dialogue

- **Context:** In the next class, the teacher asks students to work in pairs and make a short profile. Sofia chooses to show Greta a photo of her sister.

- **Illustration:** Students discussing a pair activity

- **Teacher:** Lavorate a coppie. Parlate di cosa fate, che lingue parlate e perché studiate italiano.
  - **English:** Work in pairs. Talk about what you do, which languages you speak, and why you study Italian.

- **Greta:** Studi o lavori, Sofia?
  - **English:** Do you study or work, Sofia?

- **Sofia:** Studio medicina. E tu?
  - **English:** I study medicine. And you?

- **Greta:** Studio architettura e il sabato lavoro in libreria.
  - **English:** I study architecture, and I work in a bookshop on Saturdays.

- **Sofia:** Ti piace il lavoro?
  - **English:** Do you like the job?

- **Greta:** Sì, molto. E tu, che lingue parli?
  - **English:** Yes, very much. And you, what languages do you speak?

- **Sofia:** Ucraino e inglese. Studio italiano perché vivo qui con la mia famiglia.
  - **English:** Ukrainian and English. I study Italian because I live here with my family.

- **Greta:** Che bello! Io invece studio italiano per lavorare.
  - **English:** How nice! I study Italian to work.

- **Sofia:** Guarda, questa è mia sorella Olena.
  - **English:** Look, this is my sister Olena.

- **Greta:** Che bella foto! Vive in Ucraina?
  - **English:** What a lovely photo! Does she live in Ukraine?

- **Sofia:** No, vive anche lei qui a Milano. E tu, hai fratelli o sorelle?
  - **English:** No, she lives here in Milan too. And do you have any brothers or sisters?

- **Greta:** No, sono figlia unica. Però ho una cugina qui a Milano.
  - **English:** No, I am an only child. But I have a cousin here in Milan.

### Vocabulary

| Italian | English |
| --- | --- |
| lavorare a coppie | to work in pairs |
| studiare | to study |
| lavorare | to work |
| studio... / lavoro... | I study... / I work... |
| studio e lavoro | I study and work |
| non lavoro al momento | I am not working at the moment |
| in libreria | in a bookshop |
| ti piace...? | do you like...? |
| che lingue parli? | what languages do you speak? |
| parlo... | I speak... |
| per lavorare | in order to work |
| perché vivo qui | because I live here |
| la famiglia | family |
| vivere | to live |
| la sorella / il fratello | sister / brother |
| la foto | photo |
| figlia unica | an only child (female) |
| la cugina | female cousin |

### Key takeaways: Useful phrases

- Studio... means you study; Lavoro... means you work.
- If you combine study and work, say: Studio e lavoro.
- If you are not working now, say: Non lavoro al momento. You can use an imaginary profile instead of personal details.
- Perché... gives a reason, while per + infinitive gives a purpose: perché vivo qui, per lavorare.

## Lesson 4: Interests and everyday life

- **Lesson ID:** `communication-basics-3`
- **Source file:** `courses/it/lessons/communication-basics-3.json`
- **Previous lesson:** `communication-basics-2`

### Overview

- **Subtitle:** Talking about free time
- **Description:** Learn to talk about your interests and free time, and make plans to meet.
- **Duration:** 20 min
- **Experience reward:** 50 XP

### Dialogue: Group conversation

- **Context:** The teacher puts students into small groups and gives them a card about free time. Sofia, Greta, and Luca find common interests and make a plan to meet.

- **Illustration:** Students discussing their interests in a small group

- **Teacher:** In piccoli gruppi, parlate di cosa vi piace fare nel tempo libero.
  - **English:** In small groups, talk about what you like to do in your free time.

- **Sofia:** Mi piace cucinare. E a voi?
  - **English:** I like cooking. What about you?

- **Greta:** A me piace ascoltare la musica.
  - **English:** As for me, I like listening to music.

- **Luca:** Anch'io! Che musica ascolti?
  - **English:** Me too! What music do you listen to?

- **Greta:** Mi piace il pop. E tu, Sofia?
  - **English:** I like pop music. What about you, Sofia?

- **Sofia:** Mi piace leggere e cucinare.
  - **English:** I like reading and cooking.

- **Greta:** Davvero? Qual è il tuo piatto preferito?
  - **English:** Really? What is your favorite dish?

- **Sofia:** Un piatto tradizionale ucraino: il borshch.
  - **English:** A traditional Ukrainian dish: borshch.

- **Luca:** Buono! Cosa fate di solito nel fine settimana?
  - **English:** That sounds delicious! What do you usually do at the weekend?

- **Greta:** Il sabato incontro gli amici. La domenica resto a casa.
  - **English:** On Saturdays I meet friends. On Sundays I stay at home.

- **Sofia:** Ti va di prendere un caffè sabato?
  - **English:** Would you like to get a coffee on Saturday?

- **Greta:** Volentieri! A che ora?
  - **English:** Gladly! What time?

- **Sofia:** Alle tre?
  - **English:** At three?

- **Greta:** Perfetto!
  - **English:** Perfect!

### Key takeaways: Your turn to speak

- Prepare a short introduction: Mi chiamo..., Vengo da..., Sono..., Studio... / Lavoro..., Parlo..., Mi piace.... You can use fictional details.
- Introduce yourself to a new group, share one interest, and ask someone what they like to do.
